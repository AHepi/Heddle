# example-draft

In this draft, an exploration of the utilization of classic style will
be undertaken. It is somewhat widely presumed by specialists that the
implementation of improvements in prose is achievable through the
establishment of a reader-orientation framework. The writer is of the
opinion that, in the event that a draft exhibits an absence of
concreteness, a revision should be effected.

Due to the fact that the curse of knowledge is very real, writers
frequently make an assumption that readers share their chunks. The
disinterested reader, i.e. one who is bored, will simply stop. This is
literally the death of the essay.

(A seed draft so the drafts/ root exists and a first run has something
to critique — it is deliberately bad in the book's own terms. Real
drafts land here as one note each; rewritten versions land beside them
under new names.)
