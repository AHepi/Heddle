## clarify

Read the request note (read_notes) and restate it in two parts, without
solving anything:

- What is being asked, in the requester's own terms. Quote the words
  that matter; do not paraphrase away the ambiguity.
- Why, if the requester gave a why. If they did not, write "why: not
  stated" — an invented why misroutes work.

Handle ambiguity by size: for minor forks, choose the smallest
defensible reading and label it as an overridable assumption. For
material forks — where the readings lead to different work — do not
pick; list each fork as a question with your recommendation, so the
human answers with a word.

Write the clarified restatement to a new note (write_notes). One
request per note.

Outcome: clarified.
